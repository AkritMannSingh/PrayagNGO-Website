## 🎯 About Us

**Swami Vivekanand Helping Hand (SVHH)** is a non-profit organization dedicated to social welfare, education, and community development. Founded on the principles of Swami Vivekananda, we strive to create sustainable change through various humanitarian initiatives.

> *"They alone live who live for others."* - Swami Vivekananda

## 🌈 Our Mission & Vision

### 🎯 Mission
To empower underprivileged communities through education, healthcare, skill development, and sustainable social initiatives.

### 👁️ Vision
A society where every individual has access to basic necessities, quality education, and opportunities for growth, embodying Swami Vivekananda's ideal of universal brotherhood.

## 🤝 Programs & Initiatives

### 📚 Education For All
- **📖 Free Coaching Classes** - After-school tutoring for underprivileged children
- **🎒 School Supply Distribution** - Books, uniforms, and educational materials
- **💻 Digital Literacy** - Computer education in rural areas
- **🎓 Scholarship Programs** - Financial aid for meritorious students

### 🏥 Healthcare Initiatives
- **🩺 Medical Camps** - Regular health check-up camps in slums and villages
- **💊 Medicine Distribution** - Free medicines to economically weak families
- **🧘‍♂️ Health Awareness** - Workshops on hygiene and preventive healthcare
- **❤️ Elderly Care** - Medical support for senior citizens

### 🌱 Skill Development
- **🛠️ Vocational Training** - Tailoring, electrician, computer courses
- **👩‍🍳 Livelihood Programs** - Self-employment training
- **🌾 Agricultural Support** - Modern farming techniques
- **💼 Job Placement** - Assistance in finding employment

### 🌍 Environmental Projects
- **🌳 Tree Plantation** - Afforestation drives
- **♻️ Waste Management** - Awareness and implementation
- **💧 Water Conservation** - Rainwater harvesting projects
- **☀️ Solar Energy** - Promoting renewable energy
